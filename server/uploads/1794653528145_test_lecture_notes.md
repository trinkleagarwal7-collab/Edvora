# Product Discovery & North Star Metric

## North Star Metric (NSM)
A North Star Metric is the key measure of success for the product team. It reflects customer value and predicts business growth.

### Formula & Examples
Spotify: Time spent listening to music.
Airbnb: Number of nights booked.
Uber: Weekly completed rides.

## The Retention Curve
Retention curves plot the percentage of an active cohort using a product over time. A flattening curve represents product-market fit.
