# Lecture 1: Unit Economics and LTV/CAC in Product Management

## 1. Introduction to Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) represents the total gross profit that a customer generates for a company throughout their relationship with the business.
The standard LTV formula is:
LTV = (Average Order Value * Purchase Frequency * Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is the total capital expended to acquire a single customer.
Formula:
CAC = Total Sales and Marketing Expenses / Total Number of New Customers Acquired.

## 3. The Golden 3:1 LTV/CAC Ratio
In Product Management, an LTV to CAC ratio of 3:1 is widely considered the gold standard benchmark:
- A ratio below 1.0 implies the product is losing money on every acquired user.
- A ratio between 1.0 and 2.5 indicates sluggish capital efficiency.
- A ratio of 3.0 or higher represents a highly sustainable and scalable growth model.
- A ratio exceeding 5.0 indicates that the product team may be underinvesting in marketing and acquisition growth.
